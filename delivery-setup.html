How Delivery & Setup Works

Sit back and relax while our dedicated arborists handle every detail of your fresh Christmas tree harvest and installation.

1

1. Choose Your Tree

Browse our online catalog or tag your tree in person at our mountain farm. Select your desired species, needle density, and exact height.

Online or In-Person
2

2. Schedule Delivery

Pick your preferred delivery date and a convenient 2-hour arrival window. Receive real-time SMS tracking updates when your driver departs.

Flexible Time Slots
3

3. We Deliver

Your tree is freshly cut within 24 hours of delivery, mechanically shaken to shed loose needles, bound in protective mesh netting, and transported in covered transit.

Fresh-Harvest Protected
4

4. We Set It Up

Our team brings the tree to your room of choice, gives the trunk a fresh 1/2" cut, mounts it into your stand, adjusts leveling, fills the water, and vacuums the area.

Full White-Glove Service

The White-Glove Setup Experience

Setting up a fresh Christmas tree shouldn't be a chore of heavy lifting and wrestling stubborn bolts. Our certified farm arborists ensure your tree stands proud, perfectly balanced, and hydrated for long needle longevity.

Fresh 1/2" Base Cut on Site

We cut 1/2 inch off the bottom right before mounting to break the sap seal so your tree drinks water immediately.

Tip-Proof Stand Mounting & Leveling

Positioned securely into your tree stand or one of our heavy-duty cast stands, balanced with 360° plumb alignment.

Initial Water Fill & Botanical Preservative

We fill your reservoir with conditioned water and our organic plant nutrient sachet to maximize 6-week freshness.
