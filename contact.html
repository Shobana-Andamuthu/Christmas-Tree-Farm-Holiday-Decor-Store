Visitor Essentials

Farm & Arrival Information

Helpful tips to make your trip to Pinecrest Meadows as smooth and joyful as possible.

Getting Here & Parking

Located on Highway 126 just 35 minutes east of the city. We have over 300 free paved and gravel parking spots capable of accommodating cars, SUVs, and buses.

Family & Pet Guidelines

Our 120-acre property is family-friendly and dog-friendly. Leashed, well-behaved dogs are welcome in all outdoor fields and the covered market barn.

Group & School Visits

Bringing a scout troop, church group, or corporate holiday party of 15+ guests? Contact us in advance for reserved bonfire pits and wagon tours.
