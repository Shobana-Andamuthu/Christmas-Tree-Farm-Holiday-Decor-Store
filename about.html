Family Legacy

Rooted in Passion, Nurtured by Nature

In 1984, Arthur and Margaret Henderson planted their first two thousand Fraser fir saplings in the volcanic soil of the Cascade Valley. What began as a humble family passion project has blossomed into one of Oregon's most beloved holiday destinations.

Today, the second and third generations of the Henderson family continue that proud tradition. Every tree on our farm receives seven to ten years of dedicated hand-shearing, seasonal pruning, and care before it becomes the glowing centerpiece of your holiday home.

Artisan Cultivation

How We Grow & Nurture Our Trees

Growing a prize-winning Christmas tree is an art of patience, precise hand-shearing, rich volcanic mountain soil, and pure snowmelt irrigation.

Volcanic Mountain Soil

Our farm sits at 1,400 feet elevation where mineral-rich soil and cool alpine nights create the ideal microclimate for dense needles and rich chlorophyll coloration.

Annual Hand-Shearing

Every summer, our arborists hand-shape each tree branch by branch. This careful pruning ensures uniform conical symmetry and strong boughs that hold heavy ornaments.

Pure Snowmelt Hydration

Fed by natural Cascade glacial snowmelt, our trees receive pure mountain water without synthetic chemicals, ensuring long needle retention and non-toxic safety for homes.

Our Guiding Philosophy

The Values Behind Every Harvest

From forest stewardship to community generosity, we uphold four timeless values that define everything we cultivate.

100% Sustainable

For every single tree harvested, our team plants two new seedlings the following spring, preserving our evergreen forests for generations.

Community Trees

Each December, we donate over 200 trees to local shelters, children's hospitals, and veteran families to spread genuine holiday warmth.

Family First

We treat every visiting family like our own, offering complimentary spiced cider, tractor rides, and warm bonfires to celebrate together.

Artisan Quality

Every wreath, garland, and centerpiece is hand-tied by our master florists using freshly trimmed boughs for unmatched fragrant beauty.
