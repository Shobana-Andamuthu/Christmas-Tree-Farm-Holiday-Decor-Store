The Complete Farm Experience

More than just buying a tree — a full day of winter enchantment nestled against the snow-dusted Cascade foothills.

Step 01

Tree Selection in the Fields

Grab a complimentary measuring pole and map of our 8 distinct tree fields. Wander through manicured rows of Noble, Fraser, Douglas, and Balsam firs to discover the centerpiece of your family's holiday season.

Trees from 4 ft to 16 ft grand firs
Color-coded variety markers & trails
Step 02

Harvesting & White-Glove Prep

Cut your own tree with our sanitized hand-saws, or request our friendly farm crew to cut it for you. We then haul it to the baling barn on our tractor wagons, shake loose needles, net it, and tie it securely to your roof.

Free mechanical tree shaking & netting
Heavy-duty netting & vehicle tie-down
Step 03

Wagon Rides & Fireplace Warmth

Hop aboard our tractor-drawn hay wagons for a scenic ride across the farm. Warm up beside open cedar fire pits, roast gourmet vanilla marshmallows, and savor fresh cinnamon donuts straight from our farm bakery.

Continuous scenic wagon rides
Warm cedar fire pits & spiced cider
Step 04

The Holiday Market Barn

Step inside our 1920s timber-framed lodge barn glowing under thousands of amber fairy lights. Shop freshly woven wreaths, handcrafted glass baubles, local clover honey, and custom engraved tree stands.

Live florist wreath demonstrations
Artisan crafts, candles & holiday gifts

What to Expect on Your Visit

Whether it's your first time cutting a tree or a 30-year family tradition, here is what awaits you when you arrive at Pinecrest Meadows.

Tree Selection

Over 50,000 mountain evergreens hand-shaped annually. Choose from fresh-cut staging barn or harvest directly from our scenic fields.

Height sticks provided at check-in
Field arborists to assist with sizing
Tree shaker removes loose needles

Wreath Workshop

Watch our florists hand-tie fresh cedar and noble boughs into custom door wreaths, mantle garlands, and festive table centerpieces.

Over 20 custom ribbon colors
Custom sizes from 18" to 48"
Fragrant juniper & cedar garlands

Holiday Photos

Capture your annual holiday family portraits at our designated scenic stations including our vintage 1952 red truck and giant wreath frame.

Vintage red farm truck photo set

Getting Here

Plan Your Visit & Directions

Located just 35 minutes outside the city along scenic Highway 126. Paved roads all the way to our lighted parking grounds.

Farm Address

Pinecrest Meadows Tree Farm & Market
742 Evergreen Valley Way, Cascades, OR 97401
GPS: 44.0521° N, 123.0868° W

Driving Directions

Take Hwy 126 East toward Cascades foothills. Take Exit 18 for Evergreen Valley Road and follow the festive wooden tree signs for 2.4 miles.

What to Bring & Wear

Sturdy winter walking boots, warm jackets, and a camera! We provide saws, measuring poles, carts, tree netting, and vehicle tie twine free.
